New York in Roblox

Manhattan, built by a script the moment the game starts. Every street, block and skyscraper comes from a table of numbers, so changing the city means changing a number.

The map

This is drawn from the same numbers the Roblox script uses, so what you see here is what gets built. North is up. The glowing line is the border; past it there are hills and forest so the world does not simply stop.

Each neighbourhood has its own building heights and colours. Midtown and the Financial District go up to 75 floors; the Village stays under 7.

Get it into Roblox Studio

Roblox games are made in Roblox Studio, which is free, and only Studio can put a game on Roblox. So this page gives you the files; Studio does the last step.

The quick way

  1. Download the place file: new-york.rbxlx
  2. In Roblox Studio: File → Open from File, pick it.
  3. Press Play. Watch the Output window: it says New York: building... and a few seconds later New York: done.

A .rbxlx file is a Roblox place written out as text, with both scripts already sitting where they belong.

The sure way

If the place file will not open, or you want to see how it fits together, paste the two scripts in by hand. Takes about two minutes.

  1. In Studio, make a new Baseplate place.
  2. Find the Explorer window (View → Explorer if it is hidden).
  3. Hover over ServerScriptService, click the +, choose Script. Delete the line it starts with and paste in BuildNewYork from below. Rename it if you like.
  4. Open StarterPlayer, hover over StarterPlayerScripts, click +, choose LocalScript. Paste in Neighbourhood.
  5. Press Play.

Why two scripts? A Script runs on the server, once, and builds the city that every player sees. A LocalScript runs on each player's own screen, which is the only place a box in the corner of the screen can live.

Putting it on Roblox for friends: File → Publish to Roblox. Whoever's account it is published under owns the game, so use your own.

What gets built

  • The island, from the Battery to Inwood, with a shoreline made of 16 points.
  • The grid. 14 avenues east to west, a street every 54 studs north to south. One game street stands for two real ones, otherwise the blocks are too small to walk round.
  • Fifteen neighbourhoods, each with its own range of heights and its own colours: glass in Midtown, brick in Harlem and the Village, stone on the Upper East and West Sides.
  • Central Park, with the Lake and the Reservoir, plus Battery Park, Washington Square and Inwood Hill Park.
  • Landmarks: the Empire State Building, the Chrysler Building, One World Trade Center, the Flatiron, Grand Central with its clock, Madison Square Garden, Rockefeller Center, the Washington Square Arch, the ball at Times Square, Roosevelt Island, and the Statue of Liberty out in the harbour. Each has a floating name you can see from a long way off.
  • Three bridges: Brooklyn, Queensboro and George Washington, with towers and cables.
  • Across the water: Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, New Jersey and Staten Island as lower, looser streets, with taller clusters at Downtown Brooklyn, Long Island City and Jersey City.
  • The border, a glowing force field round all of it. Solid by default.
  • Beyond the border: 1,500 studs of grass, 80 hills, 600 trees and a few islands at sea, and an invisible wall at the very edge so nobody falls off.
  • You spawn on 5th Avenue at 34th Street, facing uptown, with the Empire State Building on your right.

Knobs you can turn

They are all in the CONFIG table at the top of BuildNewYork.

KnobWhat it does
SEEDSame seed, same city. Change it and every random choice changes: which lots are empty, how tall each building is, where the hills go.
BORDER_SOLIDtrue stops you at the glowing border. false lets you walk out into the hills. The invisible wall at the far edge is always there.
HEIGHT_PER_FLOORStuds per floor. 4 makes towers feel tall. 3 is nearer real life.
BUILDINGS_PER_BLOCKAt most this many in a block. More means thinner buildings and more parts.
BEYONDHow far the hills and forest go past the border. Bigger means a longer build when the game starts.
HILLS, FOREST_TREESHow many of each, out past the border.
AVENUE_SPACING, STREET_SPACINGThe size of the grid. Everything else is worked out from these.

Below CONFIG come the tables that are the city. SHORE is the outline. ZONES is the neighbourhoods, one line each. LANDMARKS is a small function per building. Adding a neighbourhood is one more line in ZONES.
