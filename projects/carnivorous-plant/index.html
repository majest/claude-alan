Help

Catching things

  1. Bugs wander over to you because your nectar smells good.
  2. When one is inside your trap, tap anywhere to spring it.
  3. Tap with nothing in there and your trap is shut for a moment, doing nothing.
  4. A trap with something in it is digesting and can't catch anything else.

Getting bigger

  1. Digesting gives you minerals. Bigger bugs give more.
  2. When you have enough, the Evolve screen appears and you choose what to become.
  3. What you choose changes how you play, so pick the way you like hunting.

Staying alive

  1. Bugs sitting on you bite, and your health drops. Watch the heart at the top.
  2. Every plant can spit acid from its mouth. It kills a bug on the spot and you still get its minerals.
  3. An acid kill counts on the wave tab too, exactly like eating one.
  4. Eating something puts a little health back.
  5. If your health runs out, your plant wilts.

Waves

  1. Wave 1 asks you to deal with five. Every wave after that wants three more than the last, and more bugs turn up to match.
  2. Every fifth wave — 5, 10, 15, 20 — sends a giant beetle instead, and each one is bigger than the last.
  3. It is far too big to swallow. The only way to beat it is acid, and it takes several hits.

What opens up when

  1. You start with the plain ones: a wider mouth, sweeter nectar, growing larger, sharper senses.
  2. Past wave 5: quicker reflexes, stronger juices, tougher leaves, deeper roots.
  3. Past wave 20: your plant's own special power, hunting roots, and stronger spit.
  4. Past wave 30: leaf walls. Two thick leaves either side of you that crawling things have to chew through first. Your hunting roots wait outside them.

Your own special power

  1. Every plant has one power that only it can evolve.
  2. Once you have it, a button appears in the corner.
  3. It reaches much further than your mouth does — but then it needs a long rest.
  4. If nothing is in range it won't fire, so you can't waste the wait.

A sundew catches by being sticky, so it works differently from the rest.
